Class Policies

The agreements that keep every MAM class focused, fair and rewarding. You also accept the relevant policy when you apply, and we email you a signed copy.

Welcome to MAM Academy, where we strive to inspire excellence and nurture brilliance in every learner. The following policies are designed to ensure a seamless and rewarding learning experience. Choose the one that matches how you learn.

Policy for Online / Home Classes

…crafting the Timeless into Time
1
Conducive Learning EnvironmentYour learning space must be conducive for effective teaching and learning — minimal noise and distractions during class time.
2
No Picking CallsTo maintain focus, phones must be on silent mode and calls are not to be answered during class.
3
Class ReadinessClasses begin promptly. Be ready at least 15 minutes before. Delays incur a ₦500 / $0.50 fine; notify the instructor at least 2 hours ahead if unavoidable. A maximum of two late arrivals is allowed.
4
Assignment ComplianceAll assignments must be completed on time. Late submission incurs a ₦1,000 / $1 fine.
5
No AbsenteeismNotify the instructor at least 24 hours in advance via email or phone. A maximum of two absences is allowed; absence without notice or beyond two incurs a ₦1,000 / $1 fine.
6
Class ReschedulingAllowed only with 24-hour notice plus an inconvenience fee of ₦2,000 / $2.
7
CertificationA certificate is awarded after a revision and a completed examination.
8
Non-Refundable PaymentsAll payments made to the Academy are final and strictly non-refundable.
9
Daily Practice AssessmentThe Academy allocates free minutes daily to review students' practice online at the agreed time. Adherence is crucial for consistent progress.
10
Internet-Connected DevicesWe request two internet-connected devices: one to your left facing the instrument, and one directed at your face for communication.

Policy for Onsite Classes

…crafting the Timeless into Time
1
Punctuality & NotificationArrive at least 30 minutes before the scheduled start, calm and ready to learn. Arriving late without prior notice incurs a ₦1,000 fine; send notice at least two hours before class if unavoidable.
2
Class AttendanceNotify the Academy at least 24 hours in advance of any absence. Failure to provide prior notice incurs a ₦2,000 fine.
3
Assignment ComplianceSubmit all assignments on time (late submission: ₦1,000 fine). Daily progress of assignments and practice is submitted via a 1-minute video.
4
No Use of Phones in ClassTo minimise distractions, phones must be switched off or set to silent during class sessions.
5
Conducive Learning EnvironmentMaintain decorum and actively participate in all learning activities — respect and cooperation keep the class productive.
6
CertificationA certificate is issued after a thorough revision and the successful completion of an examination.
7
Non-Refundable PaymentsAll payments made to the Academy are strictly non-refundable.
8
Daily Practice AssessmentsAllocate free time daily to review the concepts taught in class — consistent practice is essential for optimal outcomes.
