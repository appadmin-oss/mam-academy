Terms of Service

By enrolling at MAM Academy you agree to the following terms.

1. Enrollment

Enrollment is confirmed when payment is received and a MAM Auditer assessment has been completed (or scheduled). Pathway placement is final once confirmed in writing.

2. Fees & payment

Tuition is billed per term. Registration ($5) is one-time. Certification & Graduation ($22) is billed at the end of your programme. Crash Programmes ($40) and The MetaMusic Order ($240) are billed in full upon enrollment.

3. Refunds & cancellations

Cancellations within 7 days of the term start are refundable minus a 15% admin fee. After 7 days, fees are non-refundable but a student may pause up to 4 weeks per term and retain their seat. Email connect@mamcentre.org to request.

4. Attendance

Students are expected to attend at least 80% of scheduled lessons per term. Persistent absence without communication may forfeit certification eligibility for that term.

5. Code of conduct

MAM students are expected to demonstrate discipline, honour and respect — toward instructors, peers and ensembles. The academy reserves the right to dismiss students who breach this code, without refund.

6. Intellectual property

All curriculum materials, recordings and the "MAM Method" framework are property of MAM Centre. Students receive personal-use rights for the duration of their enrollment.

7. Performances & media

Students may be recorded during showcases and rehearsals. Footage is used for academy promotion. Opt-outs may be requested in writing before each event.

8. Liability

MAM Academy is not liable for personal items brought to onsite classes. Students participate in performances at their own risk.

9. Changes

We may revise these terms. Material changes will be communicated to enrolled students by email at least 14 days before they take effect.

10. Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria.
