What the card was costing you
Four themes meant no consistent brand read. It duplicated the logo sitting 40px above it. And backdrop-filter:blur(20px) across a 620px panel over a full-bleed photo is a real cost on a mid-range Android — which is most of your Lagos traffic.
One primary action, not two
The old slides stacked two full-width buttons of equal weight, so neither read as the next step. Now a solid pill plus an underlined text link — the secondary is still one tap, and it stops competing.
Still intact
Pause control, reduced-motion, inactive slides out of the tab order, steering stops autoplay, 24/44px dots, live region, dot progress fill, hover labels, drag, arrow keys. The proof strip is persistent, so nothing essential lives in one slide.
