STEP 6 OF 7 · SAFEGUARDING

References and safeguarding

We teach children, often one to one. This part is not a formality, and we would rather be plain about it than bury it in a policy.

MAM Academy is committed to safeguarding children. Every instructor is checked before they teach: we take up both of your references, confirm your identity, and require current safeguarding clearances. An offer always follows those checks — never precedes them.

We ask for your referees' contact details here, and nothing else. Please do not upload certificates or enter any document numbers — we collect that evidence in person, and record only that a check was completed and by whom.
