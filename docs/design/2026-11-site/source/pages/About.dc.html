About MAM Academy

Photo — MAM students and faculty on stage after a showcase (upload)

A creative intelligence and performance academy

MAM Academy is not merely a music school. Since 2008, as the training arm of MAM Centre, it has formed musicians, performers, worship leaders, composers, conductors and creative thinkers — people who can play, perform, read, lead and think.

Students learn online or in person, through four levels equivalent to Trinity College London Grades 1–8, guided by a method of our own and taught by working musicians.

Our history →Learn more about the history of MAM Academy

Excellence across four schools of learning

Every programme draws on four schools: Instrumental, Performance, Rhythm, and MetaMusic & Consciousness. Students grow as players, performers, readers and thinkers at the same time.

Instrumental Intelligence · Performance Intelligence · Rhythm Intelligence · MetaMusic & Consciousness

Every student is heard first

Before training begins, each applicant takes a MAM Auditer assessment. We listen to how they play, hear, move and perform, and place them at the level where they will grow fastest.

Ten dimensions of musical readiness, measured on entry and reported every term.
Learn more about the MAM Auditer →How the assessment works
Photo — an assessor listening to a student (upload)

A method of our own

The MAM Method is a complete system, not just a curriculum. Five pillars shape every lesson at every level, from a four-year-old’s first notes to a diploma recital.

Technique · Rhythm Intelligence · Performance Intelligence · Creative Consciousness · Artistic Discipline
Learn more about the four levels →From Pre-Certificate to Diploma

Taught by working musicians

Our faculty are performers, worship leaders and composers who are active in their craft. The academy is led by its founder, Just-Joe Adeola, who also leads The MetaMusic Order and the monthly Power Play series.

Support that makes a difference

Cost should not decide who becomes a musician. The NGV Scholarship supports talented students for whom tuition is the only barrier.

A community beyond lessons

Every term ends in a showcase where every student performs. Each month, Power Play brings musicians, creatives and culture shapers together online — free and open to all.

Music that builds people.
