The MAM Auditer is where we discover your musical potential and find your true starting point. It looks at more than performance — we listen for musicianship, professionalism, character and preparedness, so you're evaluated fairly and placed where you'll grow fastest.

Come fully prepared, and the session becomes your opportunity to shine — for both professional and ministry pathways.
