Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026. This policy describes how MAM Academy collects, uses and protects your information.

Last updated 2026 · Aligned with Nigeria's NDPA (2023), the EU GDPR, the UK GDPR and Canada's CASL & PIPEDA

1. Who we are (Data Controller)

MAM Academy is the training arm of MAM Centre, 2 Oremeji Street, Egbeda, Lagos, Nigeria — the data controller responsible for your personal data. For any privacy request or to reach our data protection contact, email academy@mamcentre.org or call +234 704 444 6517.

2. Information we collect

  • Application data you submit (name, email, phone, age range, country, pathway choice, learning goals).
  • Payment metadata (transaction reference, status, provider) — never your card number, which is handled by certified payment providers.
  • Local storage on your device for form drafts, currency preference and accessibility settings.
  • Anonymous usage analytics — only if enabled by you.

3. How we use it

  • To process your enrollment and schedule MAM Auditer assessments.
  • To send confirmations, schedules and academy updates by email.
  • To improve the website and our educational offerings.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

4. Payment processors

We use Paystack, Flutterwave and Stripe for payments. These providers are PCI-DSS compliant and handle your card data directly. Their privacy policies apply to that data; we only receive a confirmation token.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share only with: payment processors (above), our email service provider for transactional emails, and as required by law.

6. Legal basis for processing

Under the NDPA and GDPR we process your data only where we have a lawful basis: contract (to deliver the tuition you enrol for), consent (for marketing emails — withdrawable any time), legitimate interests (to run and improve the academy, kept balanced against your rights) and legal obligation (tax, accounting and regulatory requirements).

7. Data retention

We keep personal data only as long as necessary: application and student records for the duration of your relationship with us plus up to 6 years for financial/legal compliance; newsletter data until you unsubscribe; form drafts in your browser's local storage until you clear them. After these periods data is securely deleted or anonymised.

8. International transfers

Some processors (e.g. Stripe, our email infrastructure) may process data outside Nigeria. Where data leaves Nigeria or the EEA, we rely on adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses so it stays protected to NDPA/GDPR standards.

9. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or port your data, to object to processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. Exercise any right by emailing academy@mamcentre.org — we respond within 30 days, free of charge. We do not use automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal effects. If you are unsatisfied, you may lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) or your local supervisory authority.

10. Marketing & unsubscribe

We send marketing emails only to people who opted in, and every one includes a working one-click unsubscribe link (and a List-Unsubscribe header). Unsubscribing stops marketing immediately; you'll still receive essential transactional messages (e.g. application confirmations, password resets).

11. Canadian recipients (CASL)

If you are in Canada, we comply with Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL). We send commercial electronic messages (CEMs) only with your express or implied consent; every CEM identifies the sender (MAM Academy, MAM Centre, 2 Oremeji Street, Egbeda, Lagos, Nigeria), provides a way to contact us, and includes a working unsubscribe mechanism honoured within 10 business days. You may withdraw consent at any time using the unsubscribe link in any message or by emailing academy@mamcentre.org. CASL complaints may also be filed with the Canadian Radio-television and Telecommunications Commission (CRTC). Personal information of Canadian residents is otherwise handled in line with PIPEDA.

12. Cookies & local storage

We use only essential cookies and local storage for site functionality (form drafts, currency & accessibility preferences). We do not use third-party tracking cookies by default.

13. Children's privacy

Students under 18 must enrol with a parent or guardian. We collect minors' data only with guardian consent and use it solely for academy purposes.

14. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the "last updated" date above and, for material changes, notify enrolled students by email.
