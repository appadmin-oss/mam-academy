Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026. This policy describes how MAM Academy collects, uses and protects your information.

1. Who we are

MAM Academy is the training arm of MAM Centre, headquartered in Lagos, Nigeria. You can reach us at connect@mamcentre.org.

2. Information we collect

  • Application data you submit (name, email, phone, age range, country, pathway choice, learning goals).
  • Payment metadata (transaction reference, status, provider) — never your card number, which is handled by certified payment providers.
  • Local storage on your device for form drafts, currency preference and accessibility settings.
  • Anonymous usage analytics — only if enabled by you.

3. How we use it

  • To process your enrollment and schedule MAM Auditer assessments.
  • To send confirmations, schedules and academy updates by email.
  • To improve the website and our educational offerings.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

4. Payment processors

We use Paystack, Flutterwave and Stripe for payments. These providers are PCI-DSS compliant and handle your card data directly. Their privacy policies apply to that data; we only receive a confirmation token.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share only with: payment processors (above), our email service provider for transactional emails, and as required by law.

6. Your rights

You may request access, correction or deletion of your data at any time by emailing connect@mamcentre.org. We respond within 30 days.

7. Cookies & local storage

We use only essential cookies and local storage for site functionality (form drafts, preferences). We do not use third-party tracking cookies by default.

8. Children's privacy

Students under 18 must enroll with a parent or guardian. We collect minor data only with guardian consent and use it only for academy purposes.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the "last updated" date above and, for material changes, notify enrolled students by email.
