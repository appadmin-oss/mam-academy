Chords & Scales

Pick a root, then a scale or chord — the keyboard lights up. Click any key to hear it, or press Play to arpeggiate the scale / strum the chord. A pocket theory reference that actually sounds.

Beat Maker

A full beat workstation — a playable drum kit (kick, snare, clap, hats, toms, rim, shaker, cowbell, crash) plus a Bass and Keys that follow your chosen key and chord progression, so it's melody and harmony, not just drums. Switch time signature (4/4, 3/4, 6/8, 5/4, 7/8), load a genre (Afrobeat, Amapiano, House, Trap, Gospel, Jazz…), tap the grid or play the pads live, press space to play, then bounce a WAV. Everything is generated live in your browser — works offline.
