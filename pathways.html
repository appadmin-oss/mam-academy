How the programmes work

Structure, duration & resources

Certified Musician develops competence; Grand Musician develops mastery. Both run on the same structure — the difference is breadth. The goal is not merely to produce instrumentalists, but musicians who understand performance, rhythm, theory, culture and the deeper principles of Meta Music.

Certification levels

A four-tier progression. You don't buy a level — you progress through all four, in either programme.

  • Level 1 · Pre-Certification — Foundation training leading to initial qualification.
  • Level 2 · Certification I — Intermediate competency and performance development.
  • Level 3 · Pre-Diploma — Advanced musicianship and specialization preparation.
  • Level 4 · Diploma — Professional-level training and mastery.

Standard Program

The default format for the Certified & Grand Musician programmes.

  • Duration — 12 weeks.
  • Major course — 1 hour per major course, weekly.
  • Additional courses — 30 minutes each · priced at 50% of the major-course fee.

Crash Program

An accelerated version of the standard curriculum.

  • Duration — 6 weeks.
  • Covers the standard curriculum at an accelerated pace.
  • Available online or onsite.

Supporting courses

Every candidate takes all four — any 2 are paid, any 2 are complimentary.

  • Rhythm
  • APE — Art of Performance Excellence
  • Meta Music
  • Theory of Music — Trinity College London curriculum

Learning resources

Materials are sold separately from programme fees.

  • Manuscripts — $5 each.
  • Handouts — a minimum fee applies.
  • Books — sold separately.

One-time events

Stand-alone sessions — 1–2 days each.

  • Power Play with Van. BOJA
  • Masterclasses
  • Cultural Architecture Sessions
  • Music Intensives

Registration & mode

Every student is first classified as onsite or online.

  • Registration — one-time $5.
  • Onsite & online — available for every programme.
  • Your fee — confirmed at checkout from your programme and mode.
