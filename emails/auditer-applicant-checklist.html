On Audition Day
 
16 Arrive 30–60 minutes before your scheduled audition time
17 Appropriate, neat, and professional attire
18 Willingness to complete practical tests — sight-reading, ear training, improvisation, interviews, or other assessments if required
19 A positive attitude, openness to feedback, and commitment to the MAM Code of Conduct
