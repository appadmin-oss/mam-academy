Thank you for applying to MAM Auditer, our structured entry assessment. Your audition measures both your talent and your readiness for professional and ministry opportunities — so please come fully prepared with the items below.

Your Audition
Date: {{audition_date}}  •  Time: {{audition_time}}
Venue: {{audition_venue}}
Reference: {{application_reference}}
1  •  Registration & Identity
 
 Completed registration form
 
 Valid means of identification (where applicable)
 
 A recent passport photograph
2  •  Performance Materials
 
 One prepared performance piece (song or instrumental) within the specified time limit
 
 One contrasting piece (if requested by the panel)
 
 Backing track on a flash drive or phone, with no lead vocals (if using one)
 
 Personal musical instrument (where applicable)
 
 Printed or digital lead sheet / score (if requested)
3  •  Profile & Portfolio
 
 A short personal profile / biography
 
 A brief statement of purpose — why you are applying, and your career or ministry goals
 
 Portfolio of previous works — recordings, compositions, performances, or productions
 
 Curriculum Vitae (CV) or music résumé (for advanced categories)
 
 Evidence of previous training or certifications (if any)
 
 A professional headshot OPTIONAL but encouraged
 
 Links to social media or streaming platforms OPTIONAL but recommended
4  •  On Audition Day
 
 Arrive 30–60 minutes before your scheduled audition time
 
 Appropriate, neat, and professional attire
 
 Willingness to complete practical tests — sight-reading, ear training, improvisation, interviews, or other assessments if required
 
 A positive attitude, openness to feedback, and commitment to the MAM Code of Conduct
These requirements ensure every applicant is evaluated fairly on musicianship, professionalism, character, and preparedness — not just performance ability.
